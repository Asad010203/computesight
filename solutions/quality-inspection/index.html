FAQ

Questions about Quality Inspection

How many defect images do we need?

Often a few hundred examples per defect type is enough to start, plus good parts. Synthetic data can help with rare defects.

Can it keep up with our line?

Models are optimised for your throughput; typical decisions take well under 100 ms per part.

Do we need new cameras?

Industrial inspection usually benefits from dedicated cameras and controlled lighting. We specify the right setup.

What happens when products change?

New variants are added by collecting examples and retraining, usually in days rather than months.
