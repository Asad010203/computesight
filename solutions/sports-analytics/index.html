FAQ

Questions about Sports Analytics

Which sports do you support?

Football is our most mature pipeline, with basketball, rugby, cricket and tennis also supported. Ask us about others.

Do we need special cameras?

No. Broadcast footage works; a fixed elevated wide-angle camera gives the best accuracy and is affordable.

How accurate is optical tracking?

Accuracy depends mainly on camera height and angle. We validate against manually annotated samples of your footage.

Does it replace GPS vests?

Many teams use both: wearables for training load, video for matches, tactics and opposition analysis.
