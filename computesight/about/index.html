About ComputeSight

We help cameras understand the world.

ComputeSight is a computer vision company. We turn the video organisations already capture into real-time decisions that cut losses, improve safety and sharpen performance.

There are more cameras than ever, and almost all of that footage goes unwatched. Our mission is to make every camera useful: accurate, responsible and built for the real world.

What we believe

Principles we build by.

Outcomes over models

We measure success in recovered losses, safer sites and better decisions, not just accuracy scores.

Privacy by design

Behaviour, not identity. Edge processing, masking and short retention are our defaults.

Humans in the loop

AI flags, people decide. Every system we ship is designed around the people who use it.

Ship to production

Prototypes are easy. We build systems that run reliably for years, and we stay to run them.
