FAQ

Questions about Computer Vision Services

Which cameras do you support?

Most IP cameras that stream RTSP or ONVIF at 720p or higher, plus video files, images and broadcast feeds (SRT/HLS).

How much training data do we need?

Thanks to foundation models, many projects start with a few hundred to a few thousand labelled images per class. We assess this during discovery.

Who owns the models?

We agree ownership up front. Models trained on your data for your use case are typically yours to keep.

Can it run without the internet?

Yes. Edge deployments run fully on-site; cloud connectivity is only needed for dashboards and updates.
