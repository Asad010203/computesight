FAQ

Questions about Custom AI Solutions

How do you price custom work?

Discovery and proofs of concept are fixed-price. Production work is scoped by milestone, with optional ongoing support.

What if it's not feasible?

A proof of concept is designed to answer that quickly. If the data can't support the goal, we'll tell you early.

Can our team take it over?

Yes. We document everything and can hand over code, models and pipelines, or train your engineers.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, before any data is shared.
