FAQ

Pricing questions

Why don't you list prices?

Every deployment differs in cameras, sites, accuracy needs and integrations. We give a fixed quote after a short discovery call, usually within a week.

Is the pilot really fixed-price?

Yes. Scope, deliverables and success criteria are agreed up front, so there are no surprise costs.

What does per-camera pricing include?

Software licences, model updates, monitoring, security patches and standard support. Hardware is quoted separately or you can use your own.

Can we start small?

Absolutely. Most customers start with one use case at a handful of sites, then expand on the same infrastructure.
