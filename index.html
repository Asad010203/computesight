    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Do I need to buy new cameras?

    Usually not. ComputeSight works with most existing IP cameras (RTSP/ONVIF) at 720p or higher. During discovery we check your camera angles and coverage, and only recommend new hardware where it clearly pays off.

    How long does a pilot take?

    Most pilots run for 4–8 weeks: one week of discovery, two to three weeks building a proof of concept on your footage, then a live trial at one or two sites with agreed success metrics.

    Does it run on the edge or in the cloud?

    Either, or both. Real-time use cases like loss prevention and safety typically run on edge devices on-site, while reporting and model management live in the cloud. Fully on-prem deployments are available too.

    How do you handle privacy and regulation?

    We design for privacy from day one: masking at the source, minimal data retention, on-prem processing and documented data flows that support your GDPR or local compliance work. We don't do facial recognition by default.

    How accurate are the models?

    We agree accuracy targets with you during discovery and measure them on your own footage before go-live. Reviewer feedback keeps retraining the models, so false alerts drop over time.

    Can you build something completely custom?

    Yes. Custom AI is half of what we do, from bespoke detection models to vision-language agents that answer questions about your video. If a camera can see it, we can usually teach a model to understand it.

    How is pricing structured?

    Pilots are fixed-price. Production deployments are usually a per-camera or per-site subscription that covers software, model updates and support. Custom projects are scoped and quoted separately.
